# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

People who want to keep up with friends, begin one-to-one conversations, and join lightweight live voice rooms from a phone or desktop browser.

## Product Purpose

Olly is a social platform for discovering friends' presence, messaging them, and moving naturally into live group voice conversations.

## Positioning

Olly makes a friend's current availability and shared rooms visible alongside direct messaging, so social connection can move from awareness to conversation without a formal meeting flow.

## Operating Context

Users browse a social home, send messages, join voice rooms, and manage a profile. The same core product is available on mobile and wide desktop screens.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current Flutter application includes friends, friend discovery, direct messages, voice rooms, and profile settings. Product copy and sample user data in the repository are demonstrative. Brand strategy, audience segmentation, and production content are undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Olly. The requested direction is professional, distinctive, and intentionally unlike generic AI interfaces.

## Evidence on Hand

Working Flutter screens for social discovery, conversations, voice rooms, and profiles are present under `lib/features/`. No approved logo, photography, customer proof, or production content is in the repository.

## Product Principles

- Make people's availability legible before asking for attention.
- Let an interaction move from discovery to conversation with minimal friction.
- Keep real-time activity calm, clear, and respectful of focus.
- Give desktop users a first-class workspace rather than an enlarged phone mockup.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interfaces should preserve readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, comfortable touch targets, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
